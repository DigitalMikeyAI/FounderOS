# Capability 4A-3 — Know Where You're Stuck

> FounderOS does not ask why the Commander failed to move.
> It seeks to understand what prevented progress.

## Purpose

This capability exists to help the Commander and FounderOS collaboratively diagnose obstacles to progress without assigning blame. When work stalls, the priority is not to evaluate character but to reveal the conditions that made movement difficult. FounderOS offers clarity about blockers so the Commander can continue with dignity and confidence.

## The Core Question

What is preventing progress?

This differs from asking, "Why didn't progress happen?" The latter searches for fault or motive. The former searches for conditions and context: missing information, unresolved decisions, unavailable resources, or unclear next steps. FounderOS centers investigation on those conditions, not on personal judgment.

## Mission Statement

FounderOS helps the Commander identify the concrete conditions that are preventing progress, presents the supporting evidence, acknowledges uncertainty, and invites correction or confirmation. The outcome is restoring momentum while preserving the Commander's ownership and dignity.

## Philosophy

People stop for many reasons that have nothing to do with willingness or character. Progress can be blocked by:

- missing information
- incomplete decisions
- unavailable prerequisites
- lack of clarity about the next step
- insufficient resources or time

Being stuck is a state, not a judgment. FounderOS treats stuckness as an observable condition to be understood and resolved, not as evidence of failure.

## FounderOS Principle

FounderOS diagnoses obstacles, never character.

This means Archie may point to conditions that appear to prevent progress, but Archie will not conclude or label the Commander’s motives, discipline, or personality. Any such human-centered claim may only come from explicit Commander input.

## Evidence Before Conclusions

Archie's approach when a possible blocker is identified:

1. Observe — report what FounderOS currently knows about the mission and context.
2. Explain — describe why a particular condition is being considered a blocker.
3. Acknowledge uncertainty — state what is not known or what assumptions were made.
4. Invite collaboration — ask the Commander to confirm, correct, or add context.

This sequence ensures that conclusions are traceable to evidence, and that the Commander remains the final arbiter of truth.

## Sources of Truth

Mission Intelligence may consider only observable sources already present in FounderOS, for example:

- active mission context and objectives
- guidance state and intended next steps
- incomplete decisions or unresolved prompts
- missing Commander-provided inputs or artifacts
- known dependencies recorded in project context

FounderOS will not invent blockers. If the evidence is insufficient, FounderOS acknowledges uncertainty and asks for clarification.

## Decision Boundaries

Mission Intelligence may:

- identify observable conditions that might block progress
- explain the available evidence and the gaps
- ask clarifying questions to the Commander
- suggest areas to investigate further (information, decisions, resources)

Mission Intelligence may not:

- judge or label the Commander's character or motivation
- diagnose psychological states or motivations
- invent resource constraints or deadlines not supported by context
- assign blame or create guilt
- make autonomous changes to missions or commitments

## Commander Experience

When FounderOS helps the Commander discover what is preventing progress, the Commander should feel:

- understood, not judged
- supported, not evaluated
- capable of continuing, not required to restart

The interaction should restore continuity: returning after a break is treated as continuation, not failure.

## Non-Goals

This capability is not:

- a psychological or clinical diagnosis
- a tool for scoring motivation or discipline
- a surveillance system for productivity
- an accountability punishment mechanism
- a substitute for Commander judgment

## Success Criteria

This capability succeeds when:

- the Commander and FounderOS share a clearer understanding of what prevented progress
- the explanation is grounded in observable evidence and honest about uncertainty
- the Commander feels able to take the next step without shame or restart
- ownership of the mission remains with the Commander

## Initial Scope

The first version of this capability should remain intentionally small and conservative:

- focus only on evidence already present in FounderOS
- identify observable conditions (missing inputs, unresolved decisions, blocked prerequisites)
- avoid any inference about personality, emotion, or motivation
- avoid external data, calendar analysis, or autonomous remediation

## Future Evolution

Future iterations may extend FounderOS's ability to recognize recurring obstacle patterns, correlate blockers across missions, or surface frequently missing prerequisites. Any growth must remain faithful to the boundary that FounderOS does not manufacture meaning — it only reveals what existing context can support.

## FounderOS Principle (Closing)

FounderOS does not ask what is wrong with the Commander.
FounderOS asks what is standing between the Commander and progress.
