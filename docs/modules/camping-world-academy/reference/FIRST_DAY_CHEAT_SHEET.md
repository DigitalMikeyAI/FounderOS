# First Day Cheat Sheet

## Golden Rule

Understanding the customer's needs comes before recommending an RV.

## The Five Discovery Questions

1. How do you plan to use the RV?
2. Who will be traveling?
3. What vehicle will you be towing with (if applicable)?
4. Where do you plan to camp or travel?
5. What features matter most?

## Major RV Categories

*   **Class A:** Large, bus-like motorized RV, often luxurious and spacious.
*   **Class B:** Compact camper van, easy to drive and maneuver.
*   **Class C:** Motorized RV with a distinctive cab-over bunk, good balance of space and drivability.
*   **Travel Trailer:** Towable RV hitched to the bumper of a truck or SUV, offering diverse sizes.
*   **Fifth Wheel:** Large towable RV requiring a special hitch in a truck bed, known for stability and space.
*   **Toy Hauler:** RV with a dedicated garage for recreational vehicles.
*   **Pop-Up:** Lightweight, collapsible towable camper.
*   **Destination Trailer:** Large, stationary towable RV designed for long-term placement.
*   **Truck Camper:** RV unit that slides into the bed of a pickup truck.

## Things to Never Guess

*   Never guess towing capacity.
*   Verify weights and specifications.
*   Ask questions before recommending a unit.
*   Admit when you don't know and find the correct answer.

Customers rarely remember every specification. They always remember how you made them feel.
