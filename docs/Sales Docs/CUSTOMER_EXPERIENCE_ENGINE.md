**Section 2: The Customer Promise**



Something like:



"Every customer who works with Mikey will feel informed, respected, and confident that they made the right decision."



This is important.

Because a lot of salespeople optimize for:

"How do I get them to buy?"

We optimize for:

"How do I make them confident?"





**Section 3: The Discovery Framework**



This becomes your "anti-script script."

Not robotic.

A framework.

Example:

**Phase 1 — The Story**

Questions:

"Tell me what got you interested in RVing?"

"Is this your first RV?"

"What does the dream trip look like?"

**Phase 2 — The Lifestyle**

Questions:

"Who will be traveling with you?"

"How often do you see yourself using it?"

"What matters most: comfort, space, simplicity, adventure?"

**Phase 3 — The Practical**

Questions:

"What are you towing with?"

"What budget range feels comfortable?"

"Is there anything you absolutely don't want?"
