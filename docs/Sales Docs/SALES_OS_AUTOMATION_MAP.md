**🔌 SALES\_OS\_AUTOMATION\_MAP.md**



*Draft v0.1*



**Purpose**



The Sales OS Automation Map defines the workflows where AI and automation can improve Mikey's effectiveness as an RV Sales Associate.



Automation should support the salesperson, not replace the salesperson.



The human relationship remains the core of the sales process.



**Automation Philosophy**



**Automate the repetitive.**



**Enhance the important.**



**Preserve the human.**



AI should handle:

organization

reminders

documentation

analysis

content creation

pattern recognition

Mikey handles:

conversations

trust building

decisions

customer relationships



**🤖 AUTOMATION CATEGORY 1:**



**Customer Intelligence**



**Problem:**



Important customer details can be forgotten after multiple interactions.



**Solution:**



AI automatically organizes customer information.



**Workflow:**

Customer Interaction

↓

Voice Notes / Manual Notes

↓

AI Processing

↓

Customer Profile Updated

↓

Next Action Created

Example:

Mikey says:

"Just talked to John. He has three kids, wants a bunkhouse, loves the Grand Design but worried about towing."



Archie creates:

Customer:

John



Family:

5 people



RV Interest:

Bunkhouse



Preferred Brand:

Grand Design



Concern:

Towing confidence



Recommended Follow-Up:

Send towing information



**🤖 AUTOMATION CATEGORY 2:**



**Follow-Up Management**



**Problem:**



Sales opportunities are lost because follow-ups are forgotten.



**Solution:**



AI monitors customer activity and recommends actions.



**Workflow:**

Customer Interaction

↓

Follow-Up Date Created

↓

Reminder Triggered

↓

AI Generates Suggested Message

↓

Mikey Approves/Sends

Example:

Archie:

"John has not been contacted in 5 days. His main concern was towing. Suggested message prepared."
